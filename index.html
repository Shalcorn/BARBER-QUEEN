Who we are

Owned by Ann Rieko, with style, precision, and heart.

BARBER QUEEN is proudly owned by the elegant Ann Rieko, a businesswoman whose vision has shaped the shop into a trusted grooming destination in Nairobi Town.

With over 8 years of experience, Ann has built a space where expert barbering, thoughtful care, and a calm, welcoming atmosphere come together for every client who walks through the door.

Beyond barbering, Ann Rieko is also a content creator, sharing beauty, grooming, and lifestyle inspiration with her community.

We are focused on meeting all your grooming needs to the highest standard.
